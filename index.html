    "The buyout consideration payable by the Promoter to the Investor Syndicate under Clause 7.1 shall be calculated as an amount equal to the initial Subscription Amount (INR 20,00,000,000) capitalized at a fixed, maximum Compounded Annual Internal Rate of Return (IRR) of fourteen percent (14.00%), calculated dynamically from the initial Tranche Inflow Date to the exact Date of Transfer, less any net corporate distributions or dividends received by the Investor Syndicate prior to such date.
